FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Rotaract, joining our club and getting in touch

What is Rotaract?

Rotaract is Rotary International's program for young people who want to serve their communities, build leadership and professional skills, and make lifelong friends. Every Rotaract club is sponsored by a Rotary club that supports and mentors it.

What is the Rotaract Club of Dr. N.G.P Arts & Science College?

We are a Rotaract club based at Dr. N.G.P. Arts and Science College in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu (Club ID 217835, charter date 11.02.2020). Our parent club is the Rotary Club of Coimbatore Meridian, and we belong to Rotary International District 3206.

Who can join the club?

Membership is open to people aged 18 to 30 who are students or young professionals, are committed to service above self, and are willing to take an active part in club activities. Use the Quick Application form in the Join section or open the membership application page.

What does the club do?

Our work is organised around Rotaract's avenues of service: Club Service, Community Service, Professional Service and International Service, plus District Priority Projects. See Upcoming Projects and Completed Projects for what we are doing.

How do I request blood in an emergency?

Fill in the Blood Request form in the Need Blood? section or on the blood request page. Our team coordinates with our donor network and a WhatsApp alert goes out straight away. In a life-threatening emergency, also contact the hospital's blood bank or local emergency services directly.

How can I contact the club?

Email rac.drngpasc@gmail.com or message @rotaractdrngpasc on Instagram. You can find us at Dr. N.G.P. Arts and Science College, Dr. N.G.P. - Kalapatti Road, Coimbatore - 641048, Tamil Nadu. See the Contact section for the map.
